THOI KHOA BIEU - CAO DANG Y TE HA TINH

CACH MO TREN WINDOWS:
1. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP vao mot thu muc.
2. Bam dup MO_TKB.bat.
3. Cua so PowerShell se mo va trinh duyet tu vao http://127.0.0.1:8765/.
4. Giu cua so PowerShell mo trong luc xem lich. Dong cua so hoac nhan Ctrl+C de tat.

KHONG CAN GitHub, Cloudflare, Netlify, hosting, Python hay Node.js.
HTML khong goi API truong truc tiep tu trinh duyet. PowerShell tren may se lay du lieu tu may chu truong va chuyen cho trang web cuc bo, tranh loi CORS cua trinh duyet.

TINH NANG:
- Mac dinh loc lop 19D.
- Chon ngay, xem tuan, chuyen tuan truoc/sau.
- Tim theo lop, mon hoc, giang vien.
- Tu cap nhat moi 60 giay (co the tat).
- Xuat lich dang loc ra CSV.

LUU Y:
- May tinh can co Internet.
- Neu Windows hien Firewall, chi cho phep tren mang Private neu duoc hoi.
- Neu hien loi API/404/401/403 thi may chu truong co the da doi API, yeu cau dang nhap hoac chan ket noi; bo nay khong the tu vuot xac thuc hay gioi han truy cap.
- Cong cuc bo chi lang nghe tren 127.0.0.1, khong cong khai ra Internet.
- Neu cong 8765 dang duoc ung dung khac su dung, dong ung dung do roi chay lai.
